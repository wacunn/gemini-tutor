# Skill targets (sourced)

Source: *The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 1–8: Language, 2023*, pulled verbatim from the Ministry's
Curriculum and Resources site (dcp.edu.gov.on.ca) — full text in `ontario_language_g3_g4.md` —
and the Ministry's *Language Foundations Continuum for Reading and Writing, K–Grade 4* (2026).
Quotes are exact; the "looks like in a session" notes are ours.

## Sentences — B3.1 Syntax and Sentence Structure
- **Grade 3:** "identify and construct various sentence types and forms, including simple sentences, compound sentences,
  and complex sentences containing adverbial clauses"
- **Grade 4:** "…simple and compound sentences formed with prepositional phrases and/or independent and dependent clauses"
- Looks like in a session: (1) complete simple sentences (a who + a doing, a complete thought; no fragments),
  (2) no run-ons, (3) compound sentences with *and / but / so / or*, (4) complex sentences with *when / because / if / after / before / while*.
  Parent reports he loses points on complete sentences — that's the first rung.

## Conventions — B3.3 Capitalization and Punctuation
- **Grade 3:** "…including the use of capital letters in dialogue and for words in titles, commas and quotation marks to mark direct speech
  and direct quotations from texts within sentences, and apostrophes to indicate contractions"
- **Grade 4:** "…capital letters for proper adjectives, commas to set off nouns of direct address, and quotation marks to indicate the title of a short work"
- Looks like: capital at the start, end punctuation, capitals in titles, quotation marks + commas for dialogue, apostrophes in contractions.

## Grammar — B3.2
- **Grade 3:** "possessive nouns, linking verbs and the progressive tense, interrogative adjectives and adverbs, and prepositions and interjections"

## Spelling — B2.1–B2.3 (Grade 3), B2.1 (Grade 4)
- **Gr 3 B2.1:** "…phonemic segmentation to spell phonetically regular monosyllabic and multisyllabic words"
- **Gr 3 B2.2:** "use consolidated orthographic knowledge, including position-based tendencies, to make a spelling choice…"
- **Gr 3 B2.3:** "use developing knowledge of the meanings of words and morphemes (i.e., bases, prefixes, and suffixes) to read and spell words"
- **Gr 4 B2.1:** "use knowledge of the meanings of words and morphemes (i.e., bases, prefixes, and suffixes) to read and spell words"
- Patterns named in the Ministry Continuum for the Grade 2–3 band (column boundaries in the PDF are approximate):
  /k/ spellings (k before e,i,y; c otherwise; -ck after a short vowel), -tch, -dge, FLSZ (-ff,-ll,-ss,-zz), words don't end in v (-ve),
  plural -s vs -es, irregular plurals, positional spellings (ai/ay, oi/oy, ou/ow), suffix changes (doubling for -ed/-ing/-er/-est,
  drop -e, y→i), consolidated through review; then less common spellings: -ar/-or = /er/, air/are/ear, ei/ey/eigh/ea = long a,
  ew/eu/ue, ough, soft c/g before e,i,y, ch = /sh/ or /k/, gn, gh.
- Looks like: study patterns from HIS misspellings, one at a time, inside his writing.

## Composition — Strand D (Grade 3)
- **D1.1 Purpose and Audience:** "identify the topic, purpose, and audience for various texts they plan to create…"
- **D1.2 Developing Ideas:** "generate and develop ideas about given and chosen topics… drawing on… their own lived experiences"
- **D1.4 Organizing Content:** "sort and sequence ideas and information, using appropriate strategies and tools, taking into account the text form and genre"
- **D2.1 Producing Drafts:** "draft short texts of various forms and genres, including narrative, persuasive, and informational texts"
- **D2.3 Voice:** "demonstrate a personal voice in their texts, using descriptive words and sentence patterns…"
- **D2.5 Revision:** "make simple revisions to draft texts, including replacing words and adding sentences, to improve content and clarity, using feedback from others"
- **D2.6 Editing and Proofreading:** "edit draft texts… checking for errors in spelling, punctuation, grammar, and text conventions, and proofread…"
- **D3.2 Publishing and Presenting:** "present the texts they have created… including by reading aloud with expression"
- (D2.2 is cursive handwriting — school is covering it; we don't need to.)

## Keyboarding — Grade 4 D2.2
- "begin to develop fluent keyboarding skills using touch-typing techniques" (and D2.6: "use a word processor to edit texts")
- Looks like: typing short, meaningful bits often; never long transcription.

## Oral language — B1.5 (Grade 3)
- "use appropriate word choice, including new vocabulary, grammar, and cohesive sentences when speaking…"
- Talking counts: spoken complete sentences are a stepping stone to written ones.
